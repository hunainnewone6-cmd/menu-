## Brand & Style

This design system blends the sensory, energetic soul of Karachi’s culinary streets with the crisp, streamlined utility of a premium modern ordering platform. The aesthetic anchors itself in **Modern Tactile Warmth**—eschewing generic flat delivery app tropes in favor of deep aromatic undertones, saturated spice-inspired accents, and rhythmic, rounded framing.

The target audience spans contemporary food lovers seeking authentic Pakistani and South Asian flavors, lunch-hour urban professionals, and families seeking fast, reliable, premium hospitality. The interface must evoke warmth, culinary appetite, absolute hygiene, and effortless transactional confidence.

Every surface evokes sizzling tandoors, aromatic saffron rice, and vibrant herb garnishes. Visual balance is achieved by pairing dense, photography-led culinary modules with expansive, breathable structural margins. Micro-interactions should feel snappy yet supple, mirroring the lively pace of Karachi's iconic night food streets.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from subcontinental gastronomy:

- **Primary (`#D97706` Turmeric / Saffron Gold):** Signals warmth, hospitality, and primary actions. Used for key call-to-actions, order workflows, and primary selections.
- **Secondary (`#DC2626` Tandoori Crimson):** Communicates spice, heat, urgent highlights, and price discounts. Perfect for spice-meter indicators, cart badges, and promotional alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` Mint Chutney Green):** Evokes crisp freshness, authentic herbs, and diet verification indicators such as Halal Certified and Vegetarian labels.
- **Neutral (`#1C1917` Deep Charcoal Basalt):** Replaces clinical pure black with a warm, charcoal stone foundation for text hierarchy and deep-canvas surfaces.

Canvas backgrounds default to clean, high-contrast warm light surfaces (`#FFFDFB` to `#F5F3EF`), allowing food photography and badge accents to pop. The dark mode counter-palette utilizes `#12100F` and `#1C1917` with low-opacity amber overlays to sustain culinary richness after dusk.

## Typography

The type scale marries the geometric, confident presence of **Outfit** for headlines with the open, humanist legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and navigational controls. 

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers clean culinary modernity with tight negative tracking that feels premium and editorial.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Guarantees high scanability across dense nutritional breakdowns, allergy alerts, ingredient listings, and delivery steps.
- **Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold / Bold):** Engineered with slight uppercase tracking for badging (e.g., spicy levels, dietary indicators, category filter pills).

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid anchors the desktop screen, compressing gracefully into a 6-column model for tablets and a 4-column framework for mobile devices. 

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 16px screen margins, 16px gutters, and compressed horizontal scrolling carousels for quick cuisine browsing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 32px margins, 24px gutters, two-column dish arrangements with persistent mini-cart summaries.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width of 1360px centered on screen, 64px margins, 32px gutters, structured into multi-column layouts featuring sticky navigation and cart drawers.

Use the spacing scale strictly: `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metadata badges and steppers; `space-md` for standard card padding; `space-lg` and `space-xl` for section separation and modal interiors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs **Ambient Warm Shadows** complemented by subtle layered background tints:

- **Flat/Resting (`Elevation 0`):** Canvas base `#FFFDFB` with crisp structural lines (`#F3ECE4`).
- **Cards & Elements (`Elevation 1`):** Applied to food cards and item listings. Soft, warm diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05)`, bounded by an ultra-thin 1px border (`rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`).
- **Floating Controls & Hover (`Elevation 2`):** Applied to hovering meal cards, filter pills, and bottom checkout action bars: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Overlays (`Elevation 3`):** Applied to item customization drawers, spice selectors, and cart sidebars: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.25)`.

Glassmorphic backdrops (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(255, 253, 251, 0.85)`) are reserved strictly for sticky top headers and floating bottom navigation bars to keep focus centered on appetizing photography.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Roundedness Level 2**, providing soft, approachable geometry that reflects casual dining warmth without losing structural crispness:

- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Filter Chips:** 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.
- **Dish Cards, Modals, and Promotional Banners:** 16px (`1rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Hero Containers and Special Trays:** 24px (`1.5rem`) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Badges and Quantity Steppers:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) to emphasize tap accessibility and distinct tag identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button:** Saffron Gold (`#D97706`) background, contrasting warm basalt text (`#1C1917`) or pure white, bold label typography, 8px border radius, with subtle warm glow on hover. Includes right-aligned price or chevron icon.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in Primary Gold or Charcoal Stone, hover state fills with a 10% gold tint.
- **Floating Cart Button:** Persistent pill container with sticky elevation, displaying total item count in a Tandoori Red counter badge and itemized total.

### Badges & Dietary Chips
- **Halal Certified:** Mint green tint (`#ECFDF5`), deep green text (`#047857`), paired with an authentic crescent/seal icon.
- **Spice Level:** Warm spicy crimson tint (`#FEF2F2`), crimson text (`#DC2626`), displaying stacked flame iconography (Mild, Medium, Karachi Hot).
- **Chef's Special:** Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`), deep gold text (`#B45309`), bordered by subtle gold sheen.

### Product & Meal Cards
- Structured with high-aspect food photography top-aligned (aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1), 16px container radius, subtle elevation. 
- Integrated "+ Add" quick-action pill overlapping image bottom-right corner. 
- High-contrast title in Outfit 18px Bold, followed by a two-line sensory description and spicy/dietary micro-badges.

### Quantity Steppers & Modifiers
- Compact pill-shaped control with high-contrast `+` and `−` symbols enclosing numerical count. Active state highlights with Saffron Gold.

### Navigation Chrome & Cart Drawer
- **Header:** Sticky glassmorphic bar featuring delivery address selector, real-time preparation tracker, search bar, and cart trigger.
- **Customization Drawer (Biryani / Roll Modifiers):** Slide-over bottom drawer on mobile, right panel on desktop; features segmented radio controls for spice levels, checkboxes for extra sauces/chutneys, and a fixed sticky button: "Add to Order • $XX.XX".
